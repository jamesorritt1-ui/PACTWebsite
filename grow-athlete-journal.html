How to get value out of this

Bring it to
the session

The point of writing things down isn’t the writing. It’s that in three weeks you can look back and see something you couldn’t see at the time — a pattern in what hooks you, a value you keep abandoning under pressure, a week where your sleep and your mood moved together.

  1. Before a session, skim this page and pick one entry you want to talk about. That’s often a better starting point than “how’s things?”
  2. Print or download it if you’d rather bring paper, or if you want to share a specific part without handing over the lot.
  3. Come back and update entries. They’re not fixed. Your values will shift, your reset routine will get refined, your ladder will get climbed.
  4. Re-do the baseline on the home page every month or so, and compare.

Where this lives, and what that means. Everything on this page is stored in this browser on this device only. It never reaches us or anyone else — we cannot read it unless you choose to show us. The trade-off: it won’t appear on your other devices, and clearing your browser data or using private browsing will wipe it. If an entry matters, download a copy.
