MENTAL SKILLS COACHING

Mental skills training with a registered sport psychologist — in Christchurch and online across Aotearoa, New Zealand.

A mental skills coach helps you train the psychological side of performance — attention, emotional control, motivation, and confidence — so you can bring your best when it counts. At The Performance Act, that coaching is delivered by registered psychologists, grounding every session in evidence rather than cliché.

What Does a Mental Skills Coach Do?

Mental skills coaching is applied sport psychology. We teach practical, repeatable techniques — goal setting, attention control, pre-performance routines, self-talk, and pressure management — and help you build them into how you train, compete, and recover.

Because our coaches are registered psychologists, we can also support the harder stuff: performance anxiety, confidence after injury, motivation, and wellbeing. It’s the same mental skills training used at the elite level, made accessible for athletes at every stage.

Mental Skills Coaching in Christchurch & Across New Zealand

We work in person with athletes and teams across Christchurch and Canterbury, and online with clients throughout New Zealand. Individual sessions, team workshops, and coach education are all available, along with our GROW digital learning platform.

Prefer the full picture of our psychology work? Explore our Sport Psychology in Christchurch page, or get in touch to talk through what would help most.

Need immediate support?

The Performance Act is a sport psychology practice, not a crisis or emergency service. If you or someone you care about needs immediate support, please reach out to one of these trusted services below. They are free, confidential, and available 24 / 7.

  • 1737 — Need to Talk?

    Free call or text, any time. Trained counsellors, 24/7.

    Call or text 1737

  • Lifeline NZ

    Crisis support and suicide prevention, 24/7.

    0800 543 354

  • Youthline

    For young people. Free call or text 234, 24/7.

    0800 376 633

  • Suicide Crisis Helpline

    0508 TAUTOKO. Support for those in crisis, 24/7.

    0508 828 865

  • Samaritans

    Confidential emotional support, any time of day or night.

    0800 726 666
