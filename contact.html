Need immediate support?

The Performance Act is a sport psychology practice, not a crisis or emergency service. If you or someone you care about needs immediate support, please reach out to one of these trusted services below. They are free, confidential, and available 24 / 7.

  • 1737 — Need to Talk?

    Free call or text, any time. Trained counsellors, 24/7.

    Call or text 1737

  • Lifeline NZ

    Crisis support and suicide prevention, 24/7.

    0800 543 354

  • Youthline

    For young people. Free call or text 234, 24/7.

    0800 376 633

  • Suicide Crisis Helpline

    0508 TAUTOKO. Support for those in crisis, 24/7.

    0508 828 865

  • Samaritans

    Confidential emotional support, any time of day or night.

    0800 726 666

More resources at Mental Health Foundation NZ and depression.org.nz.
