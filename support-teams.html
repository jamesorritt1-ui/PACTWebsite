TEAM & ORGANISATIONAL

Built around your people, your environment, and your goals.

We partner with teams and organisations across Aotearoa to strengthen the human side of performance. Every engagement is shaped by a proper conversation first, so the work genuinely fits your context instead of sitting on top of it.

01 / TEAMS
TEAMS & CLUBS

Team workshops and embedded support.

From single workshops to season-long partnerships, we help teams develop the mental skills, team culture, and leadership needed to perform together under pressure.

  • Mental skills workshops tailored to your sport and level
  • Team culture, values, and standards work
  • Leadership groups and player-led development
  • Pre-campaign planning and post-campaign review
  • Embedded support across a season or tournament

Engagements range from a single two-hour workshop through to long-term partnerships where we become a familiar face inside your environment.

02 / COACH DEV
COACH DEVELOPMENT

Coach development and staff support.

We work with coaching teams and performance staff to build skills, self-awareness, and the shared language needed to get the best from athletes and from each other.

  • Coach education workshops and PD sessions
  • Group programmes for coaching cohorts and networks
  • Effective coach conversations, grounded in Motivational Interviewing
  • Staff wellbeing, workload, and sustaining a high-performing team
  • Mentoring and reflective practice for head coaches and directors

Evidence-based coach conversations

Coach conversations sit at the heart of almost every development moment in sport. We offer dedicated training and ongoing coaching in Motivational Interviewing (MI), helping your coaches lead conversations that build trust, surface motivation, and support lasting change with athletes and staff.

03 / SCHOOLS
SCHOOLS & ACADEMIES

Programmes for schools and academies.

We design age-appropriate, long-term programmes for schools and academies that want to develop the whole athlete, not just the on-field side.

  • Year-long athlete development programmes
  • Sessions for first XV, first XI, and representative squads
  • Workshops for students navigating selection, pressure, and identity
  • Parent evenings and whānau education sessions
  • Support for coaches, HODs, and directors of sport

Programmes are co-designed with school staff so the content complements your existing curriculum, sporting calendar, and pastoral care.

04 / ORG CONSULT
ORGANISATIONS

Consultancy for sporting organisations.

We partner with national bodies, regional organisations, franchises, and clubs on the strategic side of performance psychology and people development.

  • Performance psychology strategy and service design
  • Athlete and coach wellbeing frameworks
  • Player welfare, safeguarding, and transition support
  • Leadership and culture reviews
  • Design and delivery of bespoke programmes and resources

We come in as thinking partners: asking useful questions, offering evidence-based perspectives, and supporting your team to build something that lasts.

05 / ONLINE
ONLINE & RESOURCES

Webinars, online sessions, and resources.

Not every piece of work needs to happen in the room. We deliver online sessions, webinars, and tailored resources for organisations with people spread across the motu or the world.

  • Live webinars for athletes, coaches, parents, or staff
  • Online workshop series across a season
  • Bespoke resource packs, videos, and workbooks
  • Access to our GROW platform for ongoing athlete development
HOW IT WORKS

From first conversation to a plan that fits.

Need immediate support?

The Performance Act is a sport psychology practice, not a crisis or emergency service. If you or someone you care about needs immediate support, please reach out to one of these trusted services below. They are free, confidential, and available 24 / 7.

  • 1737 — Need to Talk?

    Free call or text, any time. Trained counsellors, 24/7.

    Call or text 1737

  • Lifeline NZ

    Crisis support and suicide prevention, 24/7.

    0800 543 354

  • Youthline

    For young people. Free call or text 234, 24/7.

    0800 376 633

  • Suicide Crisis Helpline

    0508 TAUTOKO. Support for those in crisis, 24/7.

    0508 828 865
