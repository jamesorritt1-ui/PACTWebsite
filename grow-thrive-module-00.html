01 · The welcome

This isn't about
toughening up

Kia ora, and welcome to THRIVE. Before anything else, here's the honest pitch — the same one you'd get in the room from the clinical psychologist behind The Performance Act. This is a collaboration, not a lecture. You're the expert on your own experience; THRIVE just hands you a set of tools and shows you how they work.

"This isn't about toughening up or thinking positive. It's about learning three moves — Acknowledge, Connect, Engage — that help you do what matters even when your head and body are throwing everything at you. We'll use them in sport first, then everywhere else."

— Your welcome from THRIVE

For decades athletes were told to harden up, hide any weakness and bulldoze through. The science no longer backs that. As performance scientist Steve Magness puts it, real toughness is "experiencing discomfort or distress, leaning in, paying attention, and creating space to take thoughtful action" — the opposite of false bravado. Athletes who perform out of fear tend to perform worse.

So THRIVE trains something different: psychological flexibility — the ability to be present, open up to whatever shows up, and do what matters, even alongside difficult thoughts and feelings. That single capacity predicts not just performance, but mental health, study success and the quality of your relationships. That dual relevance — sport and life — is the whole point.

02 · Your map

Meet ACE — the home screen
for the whole program

Everything in THRIVE hangs off one simple spine you can carry in your head for life. It's called ACE, and it maps one-to-one onto the three pillars of psychological flexibility — Russ Harris's TriFlex. Three moves. That's the entire model.

A
Acknowledge
Be Open

Kindly notice and name the thoughts, feelings and sensations showing up — without fighting them or being run by them.

C
Connect
Be Present

Come back into your body and the present moment; ground and steady yourself, anchored by the part of you that simply notices.

E
Engage
Do What Matters

Turn toward what you're doing and what matters; take a values-guided action — even when it's uncomfortable.

Here's the genius of it. ACE is three things at once: a 30-second grounding skill you can run on the start line, the table of contents for this whole program, and a complete, portable model of a flexible mind. The same three moves you use before a penalty also work before an exam, a hard conversation, a job interview, or a 2am spiral of worry. One skill set, every arena.
