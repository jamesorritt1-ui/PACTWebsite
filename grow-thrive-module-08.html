Putting ACE Together

~22 minutes Practise · decide · build Private to you

01 · The move

Three moves,
one skill

You’ve built each piece separately. Now they fuse into a single, fluid move you can run in seconds — on the start line, between points, before you walk into a room. It’s the complete cycle you met on day one, now yours:

A
Acknowledge
Open up. Notice the thoughts and feelings; make room; unhook.
C
Connect
Come back. Feet, breath, anchor — land in the present.
E
Engage
Turn to what matters and take the next valued action.

"ACE isn’t three things you do — it’s one move with three beats. Open, land, act. You can run it slow and deep, or fast enough that no one even sees."

— Putting it together

The key idea of this module is the choice point. Every pressure moment is a fork: you can make an away move (fight it, avoid it, get hooked and swept off course) or a towards move (run ACE and step towards your values). You won’t always pick the towards move — nobody does. But the more you see the fork, the more often you get to choose. Let’s practise.

02 · Do it now

The full ACE run

Before you decide anything, feel the whole sequence once. Bring to mind a pressure moment — real or coming up — and run all three beats. Take about a minute.

03 · The main event · do it now

The choice-point simulator

Five pressure moments — some sport, some life. At each, you’re at a choice point: pick the response that best runs ACE and moves you towards what matters. You’ll see, in real time, how each choice steers you. There are no trick questions — just reps at spotting the fork.

Notice what the simulator is really training: not getting it “right,” but seeing the choice point in the first place. Once you can see the fork, ACE is always available — even after an away move.

06 · This week’s Mind Gym

Run ACE for real

This week your job is simple: run your ACE routine before things that matter, and notice the choice points as they come. Here’s how to make it stick, plus a daily log.

  • On the pitch

    Run your ACE routine before every session and game — even a 10-second version between reps or points.

  • Spot the fork

    Once a day, catch a choice point as it happens and name it: “towards or away?” Just seeing it is a win.

  • Life reps

    Use the full cycle in one non-sport pressure moment — before you hit send, walk in, or start the hard thing.

  • Recover

    Made an away move? No drama. The next choice point is seconds away — just run ACE and step back towards your values.

  • Diary

    Log a line a day below. Watch how quickly “open, land, act” becomes automatic.

The 7-day ACE log

One entry a day for seven days. Did you run ACE at a pressure moment? Log whether it was mostly a towards or away move, and one line on what happened. Everything saves on this device only.
