ONE-TO-ONE

Every athlete, coach, and parent sits inside a bigger story.

Individual work with us starts with listening. We take time to understand what you’re navigating, what matters to you, and where you want to grow. From there, we tailor the support to fit your sport, your stage, and your life.

01 / ATHLETES
ATHLETES

Support for athletes at every stage.

From emerging talent to established professionals, we work alongside athletes to strengthen the mental side of performance and protect wellbeing along the way.

  • Performance psychology and mental skills development
  • Managing pressure, nerves, and performance anxiety
  • Confidence, identity, and navigating setbacks
  • Return-to-play after injury or a tough period
  • Transitions: selection, debut, retirement, or a new level
  • Goal setting, focus, and pre-performance routines

Sessions can be in person across Aotearoa or online, and are designed to fit training, study, or work commitments.

02 / COACHES
COACHES

Support for coaches who want to grow their craft.

Coaching is relational work. We help coaches build the self-awareness, communication, and confidence to get the best from their people, without losing themselves along the way.

  • One-to-one coach mentoring and reflective practice
  • Coach wellbeing, identity, and sustaining a long career
  • Navigating difficult conversations with athletes, staff, and parents
  • Leadership, team culture, and selection decisions
  • Managing your own pressure, setbacks, and transitions

A note on coach conversations

Some coaches also come to us specifically to strengthen their conversations with athletes. We offer individual coaching grounded in Motivational Interviewing (MI), an evidence-based way of working that helps coaches have more effective, values-led conversations: the kind that draw out motivation, deepen trust, and support real behaviour change.

03 / PARENTS
PARENTS & WHĀNAU

Support for the people behind the athlete.

Parents and whānau often carry a lot of the invisible load in youth and developing sport. We offer a calm, practical space to talk things through and find the role that works for you and your child.

  • Understanding the pressures young athletes face today
  • Supporting your child through selection, setbacks, and big moments
  • Finding the right balance between involvement and space
  • Navigating sideline life, club politics, and tricky relationships
  • Looking after your own wellbeing as a sport parent

Parent sessions can be one-off or ongoing, and can include both parents or whānau members where helpful.

HOW IT WORKS

A simple, human process.

01

Reach out

Send us a message through the contact form. Tell us a little about where you’re at and what you’re hoping for.

02

Free kōrero

We arrange a short, no-pressure chat to make sure we’re the right fit and to shape what ongoing support could look like.

03

Work together

We agree on a way of working that suits you. Sessions are flexible, confidential, and built around your goals.

Ready to start a conversation?

Whether you’re an athlete, coach, or parent, the first step is the same. A short kōrero to see if we’re a good fit.

Need immediate support?

The Performance Act is a sport psychology practice, not a crisis or emergency service. If you or someone you care about needs immediate support, please reach out to one of these trusted services below. They are free, confidential, and available 24 / 7.

  • 1737 — Need to Talk?

    Free call or text, any time. Trained counsellors, 24/7.

    Call or text 1737

  • Lifeline NZ

    Crisis support and suicide prevention, 24/7.

    0800 543 354

  • Youthline

    For young people. Free call or text 234, 24/7.

    0800 376 633

  • Suicide Crisis Helpline

    0508 TAUTOKO. Support for those in crisis, 24/7.

    0508 828 865

  • Samaritans
