THE PERFORMANCE ACT

THE GROW
PLATFORM

Your online hub for reinforcing, revisiting, and building on the work we do together — because the real growing happens between sessions.

THE SESSION ENDS.
THE GROWING DOESN’T.

01

YOUR DIGITAL
LEARNING HUB

GROW is the digital platform where your club, your team, or you as an individual can access and revisit everything from your work with The Performance Act. It’s where the tools, the frameworks, and the resources live — available whenever you need them.

We built GROW because we know that real change doesn’t happen in a single session. It happens in the days and weeks that follow — when you’re back on the field, in the changing room, or sitting with a coach after a tough training. GROW makes sure the learning is always within reach.

02

WHAT’S
INSIDE

Self-Paced Video Courses

Learn at your own pace. Video content designed to reinforce what we cover in person, available whenever you need it.

Session Plans & Frameworks

Ready-to-use session plans that coaches can take straight to training. Structured, practical, and evidence-based.

Digital Resource Library

Articles, worksheets, reflection tools, and exercises — all in one place. A living library that grows alongside your programme.

Workshop Materials

Everything from your workshops, captured and accessible. Review key concepts, revisit slides, and share with your wider team.

Follow-Up Content

After every face-to-face session, new content appears on GROW. This is where the learning is consolidated and deepened.

03

GROW FITS
YOUR WORLD

FOR INDIVIDUALS

Access your personal resources, track your journey, and revisit key concepts from your 1:1 sessions. GROW becomes your personal toolkit — always there when you need it.

FOR CLUBS & TEAMS

Your team gets a shared space with workshop content, session plans, and resources tailored to your group. Coaches and athletes access the same materials — building shared language and understanding.

FOR ORGANISATIONS

A centralised platform for all your programmes. Multiple teams, multiple levels, one hub. Leadership can access resources across the entire organisation.

GROW access is included with every Performance Act programme. It’s not an add-on — it’s part of how we work.
